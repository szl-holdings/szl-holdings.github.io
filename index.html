The thesis

Proof, not promises.

Most AI systems ask you to trust them. Ours ask you to verify them. Every consequential decision leaves a signed, replayable trail — from the model that produced it to the theorem that governs it.

01

Sovereign inference

Runs on infrastructure you own. Paid providers stay unarmed — an explicit, honest fallback, never the default. Your reasoning, your keys, your bill.

02

Signed receipts

Every action emits a DSSE / ECDSA P-256 receipt — cosign-compatible, hash-chained, and UNSIGNED-honest when a key is absent. One signing primitive, shared by every component.

03

Machine-checked truth

The governance aggregator Λ is formalized in Lean 4 + Mathlib. We state Λ-uniqueness as Conjecture 1 — not a closed theorem. Honest about what's proven and what isn't.

04

Multi-party witnessed

A new category: 3-of-4 BFT agreement where each witness cosigns an action hash with its own P-256 key over DSSE. No single node decides alone.
